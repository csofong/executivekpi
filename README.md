# 📊 Executive KPI Reporting Template

A ready-to-use Excel workbook for tracking **Revenue Achievement** and **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)** — the two KPIs most frequently reported to C-suite executives in revenue cycle and financial administration roles.

## What's Inside

- **Instructions** — how to use the workbook
- **Data Input** — enter monthly figures in the highlighted (blue) cells only
- **KPI Dashboard** — auto-calculates:
  - Revenue Achievement % (`Actual ÷ Target`)
  - Revenue Variance ($)
  - DSO (`Total Receivables ÷ Credit Sales × 365`)
  - DSO status flag (On Target / Above Target)
  - Rolling averages

## Why

Reporting accuracy and turnaround time matter as much as the numbers themselves. This template is built the way I structure real KPI reporting for executive teams: input cells clearly separated from formulas, zero manual calculation, and a format that's ready to present as-is.

## Use It

1. Download `Executive-KPI-Reporting-Template.xlsx`
2. Open the **Data Input** tab and replace the example row with your figures
3. The **KPI Dashboard** tab updates automatically
